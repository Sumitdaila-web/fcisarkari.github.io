FCI Recruitment 2026 - Overview

The Food Corporation of India (FCI) conducts the FCI Exams to recruit eligible candidates for various categories [Category 1, 2, 3, and 4]. FCI is one of the largest Public Sector undertakings that deals with food grain and supply chain management. Established on 14th January 1965 with its first district office in Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu, the FCI has been managing various depots and private equity godowns all across the country.

For the year 2026, FCI Recruitment 2026 Notification PDF is expected to be released for 33566 or more vacancies.

FCI Recruitment 2026 Exam Pattern & Syllabus

Phase 1 Exam Pattern

Important Notes:

  1. In Phase-I, the test will be Objective type (Multiple Choice Questions).
  2. Each question will carry an equal 1 (one) mark.
  3. For each wrong answer, there will be a negative marking of one-fourth (1/4) of the mark assigned to that question.
  4. If a question is left blank, i.e. no answer is marked by the candidate; there will be no negative marking for that question.
  5. The marks obtained in Phase-I will NOT be reckoned in the final merit ranking.

FCI Recruitment 2026 Salary & Cut Off

Salary Package

Food Corporation of India offers a lucrative salary package to candidates shortlisted for the post of FCI Manager. The managers will draw a starting basic pay of Rs. 40,000/- p.m. (for a training period of 6 months) applicable to Officers in category II and they will be eligible for Dearness Allowance, House Keep Up Allowance, House Rent Allowance, and Grade Allowance.

At present, the initial monthly Gross emoluments are approximately Rs. 71,000 p.m.

When will FCI Recruitment 2026 Notification release?

The official notification for FCI Recruitment 2026 is expected to be released soon on the official website https://fci.gov.in/. The exact date will be announced by the Food Corporation of India.

What are the posts in FCI Recruitment 2026?

FCI Recruitment 2026 is expected to include posts in Category 1, 2, 3, and 4. The major posts include Manager (General), Manager (Depot), Manager (Accounts), Manager (Technical), Manager (Civil Engineer), Manager (Electrical/Mechanical), and Manager (Hindi).
